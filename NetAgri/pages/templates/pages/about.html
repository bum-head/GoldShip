ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making agricultural connections easier.

NetAgri is an early-stage project exploring how technology can help farming communities access useful information, connect with buyers, and find local agricultural resources.

The problems we're exploring

Farmers can face challenges involving agricultural information, changing weather conditions, post-harvest losses, and access to markets. These challenges can be particularly difficult when information and services are scattered across different sources.

NetAgri aims to explore ways of bringing relevant information and regional connections together in one place.

Who is NetAgri for?

Farmers

Access useful agricultural information and, in future versions, explore local selling and resource opportunities.

Buyers

Discover opportunities to connect with farmers and source agricultural produce.

Local service providers

Potentially help farmers discover relevant storage and transportation services.

Where we are now

NetAgri is currently under development. The website establishes the initial project structure and explains the problems we intend to address. Interactive agricultural features are planned for future development.

AGRICULTURE · CONNECTIVITY · INFORMATION

Building better connections for agriculture.

NetAgri is a project aimed at helping farming communities access agricultural information and connect with buyers and local services.

Learn about NetAgri

What are we trying to solve?

Agricultural productivity

Make useful farming information and cultivation guidance easier to access.

Climate uncertainty

Make relevant weather information and agricultural advisories easier to find.

Post-harvest losses

Explore ways to connect farmers with storage resources and information about crop handling.

Market connectivity

Help farmers and buyers discover opportunities to connect within their regions.

A platform in development

NetAgri is currently under development. These are the problems the project aims to address; its agricultural and marketplace features are not yet implemented.

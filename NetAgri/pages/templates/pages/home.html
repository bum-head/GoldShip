What NetAgri is for

We are working on four problems: farming information that is hard to find, uncertain weather, crops lost after harvest, and farmers and buyers who never meet.

Payments, delivery and booking are not part of the platform yet. An accepted inquiry is an agreement to talk, not a completed sale.
